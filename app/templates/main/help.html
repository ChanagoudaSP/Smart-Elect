Help Center

Find answers to common questions about using Smart-Elect

Voting Guide

  • How to Vote

    Open an active election, select your candidate, and confirm your vote.

  • Vote Security

    Your vote is recorded securely and only once per voter.

  • View Results

    Once an election ends, you can view the results from your dashboard.

Account Security

  • Face Enrollment

    If you enrolled a face during registration, it is stored securely and used only for identity features.

  • Password Safety

    Use a strong password and never share it with anyone.

  • Account Recovery

    Contact the administrators if you need to recover your account.

Common Issues

  • Cannot Vote

    Make sure the election is active, your account is verified, and you haven't already voted.

  • Camera Not Working

    Ensure your browser has camera permission and you are using a supported browser.

  • Technical Problems

    Clear your browser cache or try a different browser if you experience issues.
